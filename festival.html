FESTIVALS IN INDIA

HOLI

Holi is a major Hindu Festival of colours that celebrates spring, love, and the triumph of good over evil

DIWALI

Diwali, also known as Deepavali, is the major festival of lights celebrated by millions of Hindus, Jains, Sikhs, and some Buddhists across the world.

ONAM

Onam is the 10-day official state and harvest festival of Kerala that celebrates the Malayali New Year and the mythical homecoming of King Mahabali.
