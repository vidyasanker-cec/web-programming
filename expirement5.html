ABOUT US

The College of Engineering Chengannur was established in the year 1993, with a vision to create engineers having the drive, skill, and confidence to become the pioneers of tomorrow. The college was set up under the auspices of the Institute of Human Resources and Development (IHRD) and is recognized by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE), New Delhi. It is affiliated to the APJ Abdul Kalam Technological University (KTU).

Courses offered

The admission process at the College of Engineering Chengannur (CEC) depends primarily on the program you are looking to join. As an institute managed by the IHRD, the majority of seats across courses are allocated via centralized entrance counselling conducted by state-level bodies.
