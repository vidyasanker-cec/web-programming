ABOUT US

The College of Engineering Chengannur was established in the year 1993, with a vision to create engineers having the drive, skill, and confidence to become the pioneers of tomorrow.

DEPARTMENT

CEC is home to more than 20,00 students and 20,000 alumni with a wide variety of interests, ages and backgrounds, the University reflects the city’s dynamic mix of populations.

VISION And MISSION

To emerge as an institution of excellence that imparts knowledge through education, learning, and research for the inclusive development of society.
